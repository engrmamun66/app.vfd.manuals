## 2. Storyboard and voice-over script

Timings are targets. Each scene lists what is on screen, then the exact English narration.

### Scene 1 — Hook and problem (0:00–0:20)
**Screen:** quick montage of thick PDF manuals and a technician scrolling endlessly; cut to the app home/dashboard.
**Caption:** "Stop digging through PDFs."
**Voice-over:**
"Every drive brand has hundreds of pages of manuals. Finding one parameter, one Modbus address or one fault cause can take far too long. VFD Knowledge Base puts all of it in one organized place, and every value comes straight from the original manual."

### Scene 2 — What it is (0:20–0:45)
**Screen:** Dashboard; slow pan across the sidebar: Parameters, Modbus registers, Troubleshooting and manual topics, Library, Compare, Bookmarks. Brand/model chips visible.
**Caption:** "Parameters · Modbus · Faults · Wiring"
**Voice-over:**
"Here you get parameters with defaults, ranges and importance levels, Modbus register maps, fault and alarm codes with causes and remedies, wiring, installation and specifications. Many brands and models, organized the same way, so you learn the layout once and use it everywhere."

### Scene 3 — Registration and sign-in (0:45–1:10)
**Screen:** Register page: Continue with Google, then the form; e-mail code (OTP) step; first login; sidebar notice "FREE FULL ACCESS · … left".
**Caption:** "Sign up in under a minute"
**Voice-over:**
"Getting started is simple. Continue with Google, or register with your e-mail and confirm it with a six-digit code. New accounts receive free full access for a trial period, so you can try every feature before deciding anything."

### Scene 4 — Search and find (1:10–1:50)
**Screen:** Header search; type a parameter name; use the **@** model chip to limit to one drive; results popup; open a result. Then the Parameters table with the model tabs and Importance filter (Important / Medium / Optional).
**Caption:** "Search all manuals at once"
**Voice-over:**
"Use the search box to look across all manuals, or type at-sign to narrow the search to one model. Parameters are tagged Important, Medium or Optional, so you know what to set first when commissioning. Modbus registers show address, scaling and access, and the troubleshooting section lists every fault with its cause and remedy."

### Scene 5 — Compare and original manual (1:50–2:20)
**Screen:** Compare view of two brands side by side; then **Open original PDF** and the **Split** button (desktop) showing the PDF beside the app.
**Caption:** "Compare brands. Open the original."
**Voice-over:**
"Compare the same function across brands side by side. And whenever you want certainty, open the original PDF, or use split view to read the manual next to the data."

### Scene 6 — Highlighting, the key feature (2:20–3:25) — spend the most time here
**Screen:** Open a parameter → **Manual page** tab / **Manual** button → full-screen page image. Show, in this order:
1. The real manual page as an image, with the **cited value marked in yellow** automatically. Zoom into the yellow mark.
2. Select text directly on the page image (the invisible text layer): drag over a sentence; copy it.
3. Create your own **highlight** and a **comment/note** on the page image; show a colored translucent highlight and the note popup.
4. Toggle a note between private and public; show that the notes can be found again via a deep link that scrolls to it and flashes.
5. Close the modal with the reverse animation.
**Caption:** "Auto-highlighted evidence on the original page"
**Voice-over:**
"This is the heart of the product. We place a selectable text layer over the original manual page image. The exact line that supports each value is highlighted automatically, so out of a whole document you see immediately which part to read. You can select and copy text right on the page. You can add your own highlights and comments, keep them private, or share them as public notes. Every note has a link that jumps straight back to the exact spot. No more hunting through pages: the answer is marked, and your own knowledge stays attached to the manual."

### Scene 7 — Your workspace (3:25–3:50)
**Screen:** Bookmarks and **My categories** (folders); Settings → theme switch light/dark; resize browser or show a phone frame to demonstrate responsive layout.
**Caption:** "Your workspace, any device"
**Voice-over:**
"Organize what matters with bookmarks and your own categories. Switch between light and dark themes in Settings. The whole site is responsive, so it works on a phone in the plant as well as at your desk."

### Scene 8 — Request a manual (3:50–4:05)
**Screen:** Requests / Send request form for a new VFD manual; submit a sample request; status shown.
**Caption:** "Missing a manual? Request it."
**Voice-over:**
"Can't find your drive? Submit a request for any VFD manual. We act within twenty-four hours from Monday to Thursday, and we do our best on weekends."

### Scene 9 — Free vs Pro and payment (4:05–4:40)
**Screen:** Get Pro dialog showing Free (limited use), Monthly and Yearly with original and discounted prices and the Save badge. Then the payment step: pricing plan, amount, start time, bKash/bank details, receipt upload. Then Settings → Submit Payment showing "Pending verification" → "Active". Then the subscriber overview with the three choices: Submit payment, Switch to Monthly/Yearly, Cancel.
**Caption:** "Free to start. Pro when you need more."
**Voice-over:**
"Free accounts have limited use: a small number of comments, highlights and bookmarks, and some menus or models locked. Pro unlocks everything, with unlimited notes and bookmarks, all models and menus, future updates, manual requests and round-the-clock support. Choose monthly or yearly, pay by bKash or bank transfer, and upload your receipt. After a quick manual check your Pro access is activated. You can follow the status in Settings, switch between monthly and yearly from your next billing cycle, or cancel at any time, and the cancellation takes effect immediately."

### Scene 10 — Close (4:40–5:00)
**Screen:** logo, URL, short feature recap icons (Parameters, Modbus, Faults, Highlighting, Compare, Request a manual).
**Caption:** "vfdmanuals.softproit.cloud"
**Voice-over:**
"VFD Knowledge Base. Every parameter, register and fault, traced to the original manual. Start free today at vfdmanuals dot softproit dot cloud."
